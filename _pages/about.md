I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group** and a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)** (2024–Present). I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 with a B.Eng. (2020–2024).

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My research interests include:

- **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**
- **Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)**
- **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**

I am supervised by Professor **Junxian He**, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.

## Skills and Research Expertise

**Core research and technical domains**

- Natural language processing (NLP)
- Machine learning (ML)
- LLM reasoning
- Reinforcement learning (RL)
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness
- Model interpretability

**Research methods and applied expertise represented in my recorded projects**

- Synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale for logical reasoning and beyond.
- Analyzing perception and chart-understanding bottlenecks in vision-language models.
- Studying truthfulness representations inside LLMs, including the universal truthfulness hyperplane.
- Analyzing inner representations and in-context sharpness as alerts for hallucination mitigation.
- Evaluating foundation models with a multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite.
- Composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations.
